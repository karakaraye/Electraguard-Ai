# How to Stay Safe for Field

Your life and safety na the number one thing. No report, picture, or video worth your life.

## Safety Rules for Field
- **Run to Safe Place First:** If fight, stone throwing, or shooting start, comot there sharp-sharp. No stay there dey snap picture or argue. Find place hide.
- **No Put Hand for Politics:** Never argue with party agents or security. Never show say you like any party.
- **Respect People:** No snap close-up picture of people wey dey vex without their permission. If you dey snap crowd, stand far small.
- **Keep Your Phone Ready:** Charge your phone full and hold power bank if you get. Make sure you get numbers of your team members.
- **Emergency Wipe:** If people catch you say dem wan force check your phone, press "Clear this phone" button for app settings. E go clear all local report immediately. The ones you don send already safe for server.
- **Comot If Trouble Dey Come:** If you feel say the place no safe again, tell your team make you comot immediately. Always check with your coordinator for safety plan.
