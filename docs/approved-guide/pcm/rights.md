# Wetin Be Your Right as Observer

As accredited election observer, your work dey very important to make election dey transparent and peaceful.

## Wetin You Fit Do
- You get right to stand look as dem dey open polling unit, as dem dey accredit voters, as people dey vote, and as dem dey count result.
- You get right to stand where you fit see wetin dey happen well without blocking officials or voters.
- You get right to wear your official observer badge, lanyard, and ID card all the time.
- You get right to write note and write the time wetin happen take place.
- You fit gently ask polling officials question if dem no dey attend to voter that time.

## Wetin You No Must Do
- Never touch ballot paper, ballot box, or any election material at all.
- Never tell voter who dem go vote for or ask who dem vote for.
- Never wear party cap, party cloth, or badge of any political party.
- Never command election officials or disturb their work.
- Never announce result or speak for INEC.

How things dey happen fit change small from one center to another. If you no sure of wetin dem allow for your station, check with your coordinator.
