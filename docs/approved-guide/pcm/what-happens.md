# Wetin Dey Happen to Your Report

Every report wey you send through ElectraGuard AI dey follow clean and safe road to make election clean.

## Road Wey Your Report Dey Pass

1. **E Dey Save Inside Your Phone First:**
   When you press send, the report dey save and lock inside your phone first. Even if network no dey, nothing go lost.
2. **E Dey Upload Once Network Come:**
   As soon as your phone catch mobile network or Wi-Fi, the outbox go upload your report straight to central situation room.
3. **Situation Room Review:**
   Reviewers wey train well go read your report. AI go suggest category and how e take urgent, but real human being must check and decide on top every report.
4. **Question for More Gist:**
   If the reviewer need more information (like which ward or whether officials later show), dem go send question straight to your app. You go see alert to reply.
5. **Join Gist Together & Clean Secret:**
   Reports wey dem verify go join other reports from that same area to see pattern. Dem dey clean all names of people and car number to protect your privacy.
6. **Public Transparency:**
   Verified reports dey show for public dashboard as total number. The location dey stop for LGA level make nobody fit trace who report.

For more information about how your organization dey work, check with your coordinator.
