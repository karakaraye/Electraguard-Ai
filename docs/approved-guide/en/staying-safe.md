# Staying Safe

Your safety and well-being are always the top priority. No observation report, photograph, or video is worth putting your life or health in danger.

## Safety Rules in the Field
- **Move to Safety First:** If fighting, shouting, stone-throwing, or gunfire breaks out, step back immediately. Do not stay to take pictures or argue. Find a safe shelter.
- **Remain Strictly Neutral:** Never argue with party agents, security personnel, or voters. Never wear party symbols or show personal political preferences.
- **Respect Privacy & Local Comfort:** Do not take aggressive close-up photos of agitated crowds or individuals without consent. When taking photos of general queues, keep a polite distance.
- **Stay Connected:** Keep your mobile phone charged. Carry an external power bank if you have one. Agree on regular check-in times with your team.
- **Emergency Wipe:** If you are stopped and threatened with a hostile physical search of your phone, you can use the "Clear this phone" button in the app settings to wipe local stored reports instantly. Your submitted reports are already safe in the central database.
- **Leave When In Doubt:** If the security atmosphere at a polling center deteriorates, inform your team and leave the area promptly. Always check with your coordinator for emergency evacuation protocols.
