# What Happens to Your Report

Every report you submit through ElectraGuard AI follows a secure, careful path to support election accountability.

## The Journey of a Report

1. **Saved Safely on Your Phone:**
   When you tap submit, your report is encrypted and saved immediately in your phone's local outbox. Even if there is no mobile network, your report is never lost.
2. **Automatic Secure Upload:**
   As soon as your phone reconnects to a cellular or Wi-Fi network, the encrypted outbox sends your report directly to the central situation room.
3. **Situation Room Review:**
   Trained verification analysts review your report. An AI assistant suggests initial category and urgency labels to help prioritize urgent threats, but a human analyst always checks and decides on every report.
4. **Clarification Requests:**
   If a reviewer needs more detail (for example, confirming a specific ward number or whether officials arrived later), they will send a formal question directly to your app. You will see a notification to reply.
5. **Corroboration and Aggregation:**
   Verified reports are grouped with other reports from the same area to identify regional patterns. Names of individuals and personal vehicle plates are redacted to protect everyone's privacy.
6. **Public Transparency:**
   Verified observations are published on public transparency dashboards in aggregate numbers. Locations are grouped by local government area (LGA) so that individual informants can never be identified.

For organizational reporting guidelines or specific escalation pathways, check with your coordinator.
