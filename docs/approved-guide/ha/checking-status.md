# Yadda Ake Duba Halin da Rahotonka Yake Ciki

ElectraGuard AI yana tabbatar da cewa kowane mai aiko da rahoto ya sami shaidar karba kuma yana iya bin diddigin yadda ake gudanar da shi.

## Lambar Shaidar Karba (Receipt Code)
A duk lokacin da ka tura rahoto cikin nasara, tsarin yana baka wata lamba ta musamman (misali: `REC-KN-7492` ko `REC-LG-1024`). Ajiye wannan lambar da kyau.

## Dubawa ta Manhajar Waya
Idan ka bayar da rahoto ta manhajar intanet ta waya:
1. Taba **Rahotannina** a babban shafin manhaja.
2. Zabi kowane rahoto domin ganin matakinsa:
   - **An Karba (Received):** Ya isa uwar garke, yana jiran mai bincike.
   - **Ana Dubawa (In Review):** Mai bincike yana duba bayanin da hoton da ka tura yanzu.
   - **Ana Bukatar Karin Bayani (Needs Clarification):** Mai bincike ya aiko maka da tambaya. Bude rahoton ka bayar da amsa.
   - **An Rufe (Closed):** An kammala bincike an kuma rubuta sakamako (An Tabbatar, Hujja Ba Ta Isa Ba, ko Ya Wuce Iyaka).

## Dubawa ta Hanyar Sakon Waya (SMS) ko Karamar Waya
Idan ka ba da rahoto ta USSD (`*384*24#`) ko SMS:
- Za ka karbi sakon SMS dauke da lambar shaidarka.
- Don duba matsayin a kowane lokaci, tura kalmar `STATUS` tare da lambar shaidarka (misali: `STATUS REC-LG-1024`) zuwa gajeruwar lambar sadarwar.
- Haka kuma zaka iya shigar da lambar shaidar a shafin yanar gizo a `electraguard.ng/p`.

Idan matsayin rahotonka bai canza ba ko ka rasa lambarka, bincika tare da kodinetanka.
