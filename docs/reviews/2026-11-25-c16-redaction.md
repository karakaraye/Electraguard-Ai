# C16 Review: Automatic Redaction of Personal Details

Branch `backend/c16-redaction`, on top of `production-readiness`. Backend only; no `frontend/` or `frontend/lib/api/` paths changed.

## What changed

**Privacy & Redaction Core (`backend/privacy/redactor.py`).**
- Ported concepts from Trace (`intelligence/Redactor.java`, `intelligence/NameAddressFinder.java`, `intelligence/CustomerTokens.java`) into Python/Django.
- Redacts sensitive personal data before display, logging, export, or processing:
  - **Phone numbers:** Nigerian phone numbers (+234/0 followed by 7, 8, or 9, then 0 or 1, then 8 digits) with arbitrary spaces, dashes, or parentheses.
  - **Emails:** Standard RFC-compliant email patterns.
  - **National Identity Numbers (NIN):** 11-digit identification numbers proximate to NIN keywords.
  - **Bank Verification Numbers (BVN):** 11-digit verification numbers proximate to BVN keywords.
  - **Voter Card Numbers (VIN / PVC):** 19 alphanumeric characters proximate to VIN or PVC keywords.
  - **Bank Account Numbers:** 10 digits proximate to account keywords.
  - **Personal Names:** Title words (*Mr, Mrs, Alhaji, Hajiya, Chief, Hon, Dr, Barr, Engr, Pastor, Imam, Oga, Mama, Mallam, Comrade, Oba, Igwe, Mazi, Arakunrin*, etc.) followed by capitalized name tokens, plus a curated list of 575+ Nigerian names across major ethnolinguistic groups (Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Edo, Urhobo, Ijaw, Efik/Ibibio, Tiv) in `backend/privacy/data/names.txt`.
  - **Addresses:** Street numbers with street keywords (*street, road, close, avenue, crescent, way, drive, lane, express, rd, st*).
- **HMAC Tokens:** Redacted values are replaced with typed tokens of the form `[KIND_xxxx]` (e.g. `[PHONE_3f9a]`, `[NAME_7c21]`). Suffixes are computed as the first 4 hex digits of HMAC-SHA256 of the normalized value using the organization's secret key. This guarantees token stability within an organization (enabling correlation across reports) while isolating tokens across organizations.
- Existing tokens (`\[[A-Z0-9]+_[0-9a-f]{4,6}\]`) are preserved and protected from re-tokenization.

**Model Fields & Migrations.**
- Added `auto_redacted_description` (`TextField`, null=True, blank=True) and `auto_redactions` (`JSONField`, default=list, blank=True) to `Report`.
- Added `auto_redacted_body` and `auto_redactions` to `CitizenReply` (`reports`) and `Post` (`discussions`).
- Migrations:
  - `reports/migrations/0016_citizenreply_auto_redacted_body_and_more.py`
  - `discussions/migrations/0002_post_auto_redacted_body_post_auto_redactions.py`

**Intake & Processing Hooks.**
- Auto-redaction executes synchronously on create/update:
  - Observer incident, checklist, and result-sheet reports (`persist_report` in `reports/v1_views.py`).
  - Observer messages (`send()` in `reports/conversation.py`).
  - Citizen replies (`reply_to_question()` in `reports/citizen_views.py`).
  - Ward discussion posts (`post_or_flag()` in `discussions/views.py`).
- Downstream processing (triage classifier, urgency scorer, duplicate detector, cluster detection, public snapshots, and reviewer queues) reads safe text via `safe_text(obj)`: manual redaction -> auto redaction -> raw text.
- Original raw text is never output in logs.

**Contracts & Endpoints (G16).**
- **REP-03:** Observer receives their original text in `description` alongside `redaction: { automatic: true, count: ..., kinds: [...] }`.
- **CASE-01:** Reviewers receive redacted text in `description`, `original_available: true`, and the `redaction` metadata block.
- **CASE-06 (`POST /api/v1/cases/{ref}/original` and `POST /api/v1/review/cases/{ref}/original`):**
  - Requires `{ "reason": "..." }` (1–500 characters).
  - Restricts access strictly to the assigned reviewer (HTTP 403 otherwise).
  - Rate limited to 30 requests/hour per reviewer.
  - Returns `{ "description": "..." }` with `Cache-Control: no-store`.
  - Records an audit event `case.original_viewed` via `staff.audit.record()` and a readable `OriginalAccess` record with the reason.

**Management Command.**
- `python manage.py redact_existing [--batch-size 500] [--dry-run]` to backfill auto-redactions across historical reports, citizen replies, and discussion posts.

## Verification

- **Unit & Integration Tests:**
  - 14 tests in `privacy.tests`: phone/email/NIN/BVN/VIN/account/address/name redactions, HMAC token stability across identical inputs, cross-organization token isolation, zero leakage in logs, contract payloads (REP-03, CASE-01), CASE-06 access restrictions (assigned reviewer required, audit event generated, rate limit enforced), and `safe_text` priority.
  - Ran `accounts`, `reports`, `privacy`: all 38 tests pass on PostgreSQL 18.
- **Evaluation (`docs/eval/redaction.md`):**
  - Generated and evaluated a synthetic benchmark of 310 reports across English, Naijá, Hausa, Yorùbá, and Igbo (62 items per language, `docs/eval/data/redaction-set.jsonl`).
  - Overall Recall: **100.0%** (target ≥95% for IDs/phones, ≥85% for names).
  - Overall Precision: **99.7%** (target ≥90%).
  - Zero PII leaks detected.
