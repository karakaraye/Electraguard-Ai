Check-ins are not reports. They never enter the review queue and never count in public results. "No" answers only raise the priority of matching cases, which the backend does as a cluster boost.
Safety is red, nothing else is. Logistics and Instruction are neutral Tags; only a Safety notice carries the danger badge, and only after a second coordinator approves it.
Privacy. The LGA card and the rumour evidence box both show "fewer than 5" for any count under 5, exactly like the public results page. Coordinators see the raw ward counts because they already manage those units.
Content check. Free text is checked for polling unit codes, phone numbers, emails, links, personal names and party or candidate names. Every reason is listed at once, and the offending words are marked in place.
