Your rights at the polling unit

Approved 20 Nov 2026Version 2026.1For voters and observers

Voter rights

Every registered voter has the right to cast a ballot in secret and without intimidation.

  • Arrive at your designated polling unit before accreditation ends.
  • Present your voter card to the official for BVAS verification.
  • If your fingerprint fails, facial recognition must be attempted.
  • You are entitled to see that the ballot box is empty before voting begins.

Never accept money or gifts in exchange for your vote. Report any pressure to our team.

Accreditation

Officials check your voter card and confirm who you are before you can vote.

Privacy notice

What ElectraGuard keeps, what it never collects, and what you can do. In plain words.

Last updated 20 Nov 2026

What we never collect

Your name

Citizens and observers do not give a name.

GPS location

The app never reads it.

Your number

For SMS and USSD it is kept only as a scrambled code.

What is made public

Only totals of reviewed reports, by state, type and hour, published at least 60 minutes later. Any number below 5 is shown as "fewer than 5". Never your words, photos, polling unit, ward, receipt code or observer code.
